# Flow audit, 24 November 2026

Four audits drove every journey in the prototype in a headless browser and read the code behind it: the listing journey, the sale and money journey, messages and approvals, and rentals, office and the rebuilt pages. Together they raised 10 blockers, about 75 majors and about 60 minors. This note records what was found, what changed, and what is left for the build.

## Product rules that were being broken (all fixed)

- Drafts GeeVee promised on a call, or that the agent asked for in the GeeVee bar, were hidden from Today and only reachable behind the bell. They are on Today now. A separate "exempt" flag keeps them clear of the seller-lead withdrawal rule.
- Seller leads inside or past the 24-hour window ranked below routine replies and could sit beyond the 25 cards Today showed. They now come straight after offers, and the full queue pages 25 at a time.
- An authority could be sent for signing from Today with required fields blank. The Today approval now runs the same completeness check as the envelope.
- A typed request such as "book Helen's appraisal Thursday 2pm" marked the lead as called. It books the appraisal and says the call is still owed. With several properties it asks which one; with an appraisal already booked it says so.
- "approve all" from the bar approved drafts the agent had never seen. Batch approval covers visible drafts only.
- Opening an open home session checked in every past enquirer as a WARM attendee, so closing it texted them all and gave the vendor false numbers. Expected people now sit in their own list with an Arrived button.
- A Contract of Sale could be signed on the signing page while it was still an unapproved draft. The signing page and the demo signer refuse until the tap on Today.
- The bell's Review modal approved protected (A3) items with one tap. It now shows the same second look as Today.
- Preview on any email or portal reply card crashed. Fixed.
- GeeVee auto-answered an open home question with a hard-coded time. It now reads the open home record, only answers open-home questions (a building inspection or access request goes to the agent), writes its reply into the conversation, marks the enquiry answered, and the "GeeVee answered" panel with its off switch renders on Today.
- A personal assistant could approve A2 sends. Every approve button, including batch approval, becomes "Ask Caleb" for the assistant. The agent role no longer reaches Agents, Forecast or Analytics.

## Wrong outcomes and contradictions (fixed)

- Two offer flows ran side by side. The Today offer card's Present, Accept, Decline, Counter and Best and final now use the sale journey: one protected present card, the vendor decision dialog, the best and final dialog.
- Best and final told buyers a deadline 11 hours out (UTC written into a local field), did not withdraw the present approval, and its task outlived the sale. All three fixed.
- Commission was price guide times fee on some pages and settlement figures on others. One formula everywhere: the sold price when there is one, otherwise the guide. Seeded settlements carry their price, purchaser, contract date and deposit.
- Office flagged every live listing as missing vendor ID while the record held the sighted ID. Both names of the field are read.
- Today compared the whole office's commission with the agent's personal target. It compares the agent's own.
- Audit trail credited agent decisions to GeeVee, duplicated drafts, and lost rows on Undo. Rows carry who tapped under the role in use, duplicates are gone, and Undo keeps the trail.
- Approved replies never appeared in the conversation and the Messages page drafted a different reply. One draft; approved replies are appended to the thread and the enquiry is marked replied.
- Approving the proposal email changed nothing on the deal. Sends are written to the property activity and the contact history; a proposal clears the risk and sets a chase task.
- Transcript parsing booked a "building and pest inspection" as a private viewing. Excluded.
- An inspection request for Saturday morning was booked into Friday. Requests match the day and time asked for, or a text offering a private inspection goes to Today.
- Post-visit texts said "today" for a session days old and two templates competed. One template names the day; duplicates are guarded.
- Protected cards were all labelled "Proposal" or "Authority". Cards now say Lease, Contract of Sale, Offer form, Go live, Offer, Neighbours, Campaign.
- The appraisal confirmation text pointed at a time that had passed. The appraisal is tomorrow 10:30.
- "Appraisal ready" cards appeared for every appraisal-stage deal before the visit, with invented comparables, and never left. Packs are seeded only after the visit and are superseded when the deal moves on. The pack's range reads the record.
- The journey strip ignored appraisals already in the diary. It reads any appraisal event.
- The conveyancer email was addressed to the vendor. It goes to the conveyancer named on the authority.
- Go-live left stale vendor texts (confirm appraisal, photo time) waiting. They are withdrawn when the listing goes live.
- Enquiry counts differed on every screen. One count (enquiry records plus unlinked conversations) is used by the property page, the reports and the bar.
- Rentals: an arrears notice could be drafted again and again (now one per week, the row says "Notice on Today"); offering a renewal removed the lease end from the roll (it now says "renewal offered, waiting on the renter"); a lease that had not started showed an inspection due (none in the first three months); an applicant became a marketable buyer with invented permission (no marketing, no import history, email collected for signing); lease documents showed "order undefined" and no property.
- Legal wording: Victoria does not require bidder registration at auction (the bidder list is Collings practice); bond is one month's rent; renewals go out 100 days ahead so the 90 days' notice of a rent change holds; arrears wording matches the Act; the prescribed rental application form and the rental bidding ban are noted. The Property Price Statement preview lists the key features of the property and of each comparable, and comparables must come from the same or a neighbouring suburb before the go-live gate passes.
- Seeded data: sellers no longer receive threads about other people's listings; seeded notes and reminders follow the deal's stage; a notification no longer contradicts the lead list; names keep their capitals in headings; the landlord report that was the vendor report is gone; sales near Heidelberg Heights exist so the CMA has comparables.
- Phone: the GeeVee bar no longer covers the dock; card menus and chips stay on screen; selecting a conversation scrolls to it.

## Not changed (for the build, not the prototype)

- The GeeVee bar understands a fixed set of phrases. New in this pass: recording an offer, best and final, presenting offers, the vendor's decision, settlement dates, the trust ledger. It still does not understand "reply to X that ...", "push everything from X to tomorrow" or "what did I approve today". In production this is a language model with the playbooks; the prototype's regex matcher is a stand-in.
- Suggested replies are template-based. They now acknowledge confirmations (no card), send the offer form, quote the advertised range and the next open, and handle "anything else like this". Everything else still gets a general reply.
- "View as agent" shows a banner only; the pages still show Caleb's records. The copy says so.
- Message times in the demo data are generated; the audit trail stamps seeded rows at load. Cosmetic.
- Minor items left as they were: duplicate suburb mentions in buyer match rows, the "Back" button inside "More" on two analytics pages, breadcrumb group for public pages, Jordan Park's crowded demo history, the Boxing Day auction date in one generated journey, the hero photo choice after delivery.

## Verified against Consumer Affairs Victoria (24 November 2026)

- Property Price Statement replaced the Statement of Information from 1 October 2026 (auctions from 16 October 2026). It lists key features of the property and of the three most comparable sales (type, bedrooms, bathrooms, car spaces, internal area, land size). The sold price is published within 7 days of the sale going unconditional and kept for 18 months.
- The reserve is published as a single figure at least 7 days before an auction or fixed-date sale; a change restarts the 7 days.
- Victoria has no bidder registration requirement; bidders are identified on request. Auction rules are displayed 30 minutes before.
- Rentals since 25 November 2025: 90 days' notice of a rent increase and of most notices to vacate; no-fault evictions ended; rental bidding banned. Unpaid rent: a notice to vacate once 14 days of rent is owed, with 14 days' notice. Prescribed application form since 31 March 2026. Renters may pay the bond straight to the RTBA since 9 September 2026. Bond claims need advance notice with evidence since 13 October 2026.
- AML/CTF obligations for real estate agents started 1 July 2026: verify the vendor's identity before providing estate agency services.
